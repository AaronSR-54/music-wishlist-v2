Novedades de la 0.24.0:

<en-US>
What's new in 0.24.0:
• On Android notifications are system-managed: the app asks for permission on first launch and the in-app switch is gone.
• "Notification settings" button in Profile, with an icon and always available.
• Release alerts arrive in the app's own channel.
• Recent releases are detected more reliably, and notifications are isolated per user.
• Profile no longer overlaps the status bar.
• Internal fixes.
